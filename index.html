Our story

Why we opened IndiVisual Eyes

IndiVisual Eyes is a small optical shop on K Street in downtown Sacramento. Most days the owner is the one who greets you, pulls frames off the wall, and cuts your lenses at the bench in back.

We started the shop because the choice in eyewear had narrowed. The big chains carry the same dozen frames, cut lenses off site, and move you through in twenty minutes. Frames are the thing people notice first about your face, and they deserve more attention than that.

So we built the opposite. A short, considered wall — Lindberg, Silhouette, Prada, Gucci, Persol — chosen because they are made well and wear well, not because a distributor pushed them. An on-site lab, so lenses are cut, edged and mounted here, often the same day, including rimless while you wait.

We take the time to get it right. You put a frame on, we mark exactly where your eyes sit, and we fit it by hand before you leave. If something bends or loosens later, you bring it back and we fix it. That is the whole idea: frames as wardrobe, fitted by someone who will still be here next year.
